Support

We're a small team, so replies can take a day or two.

Email us any time at:

support@example.com

Please include your username and a short description of what's going on (screenshots help).

What to email us about

  • Bugs and problems: something broken, slow or confusing.
  • Reports: content that breaks the rules. For anything involving child safety, email us right away with the invite link or username.
  • Ban appeals: tell us what happened and why you think it should be lifted.
  • Server creation access: see below.
  • Account help or deleting your account.

Questions

How do I create my own server?

New accounts can join servers right away, but creating one needs approval from a site admin. Make an account, then email us your username and a line about what the server is for, and we'll switch it on.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no paid tiers.

Do I need a phone number or email?

No. You sign up with just a username and a password. That also means we can't reset a forgotten password by email, so keep it somewhere safe.

Is it "uncensored"?

We moderate lightly, not never. Normal arguments, swearing and dark humor are fine. A short list of things is banned everywhere (see the Terms).

How big can uploads be?

Up to 20 MB per file, 5 files per message.

Someone is bothering me. What can I do?

Tell the server's moderators or owner first; they can kick or ban people from their server. If it's serious or they can't help, email us.

Is StandyCord affiliated with Discord?

No. StandyCord is an independent project with no connection to Discord Inc.
